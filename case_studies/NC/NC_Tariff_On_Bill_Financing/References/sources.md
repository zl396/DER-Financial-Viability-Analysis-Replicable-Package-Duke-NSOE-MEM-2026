## 1. Foundational policy / methodology

### Stanton & Sklar (2020) — *NRRI Insights*
**Citation:** Stanton, T. & Sklar, S. (Jan 2020). "Utility Tariff On-Bill Financing: Provisions and Precautions for Equitable Programs." NRRI Insights, January 2020. With reaction by Howat, J. & Wein, O. (National Consumer Law Center). Published by the National Regulatory Research Institute (NRRI), the research arm of NARUC.
**Access:** https://www.naruc.org/nrri/nrri-library/research-reports/ (search title) — Copyright NRRI 2020.
**Role in analysis:** Foundational distinction between OBF / OBR / TOB program types. Source of Table 1 (program design elements). NCLC reaction informed the low-income consumer-protection framing.

### National Consumer Law Center (2023) — Risks for Low-Income Consumers
**Citation:** National Consumer Law Center (Feb 2023). "Tariff-based On-Bill Financing: Assessing the Risks for Low-Income Consumers."
**Access:** https://www.nclc.org/resources/?fwp_search=tariff-based-on-bill-financing
**Role:** Counterweight to TOBF advocacy — disconnection risk, displacement of zero-cost programs (WAP), the case for Net Bill Neutrality guarantees.

### Cadmus Group (2019) — Minnesota Feasibility Study
**Citation:** Cadmus Group (Aug 2019). "Tariffed On-Bill Financing Feasibility: Assessment of Innovative Financing Structures for Minnesota." Prepared for Energy Transition Lab, Institute on the Environment, University of Minnesota.
**Access:** https://environment.umn.edu/research/energy/ (search title)
**Role:** State-level feasibility framework — adapted to NC context for our cash flow assumptions.

### Collaborative Efficiency & Michigan Saves (2015) — OBF Primer
**Citation:** Collaborative Efficiency & Michigan Saves (Sept 2015). "Overcoming the Barriers to Energy-Related Investments with an On-Bill Financing Program: A Primer for Municipal Utilities and Electric Cooperatives." With support from EESI, C.S. Mott Foundation, Council of Michigan Foundations, Joyce Foundation.
**Access:** https://www.eesi.org/obf or https://michigansaves.org/
**Role:** Program design checklist for muni utilities and co-ops — informed our stakeholder mapping.

## 2. U.S. DOE & federal program documentation

### Better Buildings / DOE — Clean Energy for Low Income Communities Accelerator (CELICA)
**Citation:** U.S. Department of Energy, Better Buildings Initiative (n.d.). "Issue Brief: Low-income Energy Efficiency Financing through On-Bill Tariff Programs." Clean Energy for Low Income Communities Accelerator (CELICA).
**Access:** https://betterbuildingssolutioncenter.energy.gov/ (CELICA partner page)
**Role:** Federal framing of TOB tariffs as a credit-blind, rental-friendly mechanism.

### Environmental and Energy Study Institute (EESI) — Interactive Map
**Citation:** Environmental and Energy Study Institute. "Interactive Map of Utilities with On-Bill Financing Programs."
**Access:** https://www.eesi.org/obf/map
**Role:** Inventory of which 110+ utilities offer some form of OBF — used to scope the NC peer set.

### U.S. DOE / Energy.gov — Bill Financing and Repayment Programs
**Citation:** U.S. Department of Energy, Office of State and Community Energy Programs.
**Access:** https://www.energy.gov/eere/slsc/bill-financing-and-repayment-programs
**Role:** Federal definitions and program-type taxonomy.

## 3. Live program references (existing TOB / PAYS deployments)

### Roanoke Electric Cooperative (NC) — Upgrade to $ave
**Citation:** Southern Environmental Law Center. "Members Save Money While Saving Power: Roanoke Electric Cooperative Leads the Way with a Debt-Free On-Bill Program for Energy Efficiency."
**Access:** https://www.southernenvironment.org/ (search "Roanoke Electric") and https://www.roanokeelectric.com/upgrade-to-save/
**Role:** **Closest direct analog** — NC-jurisdiction TOB program already operating on a member co-op. Tariff structure modeled directly on PAYS®.

### Ouachita Electric Cooperative (Arkansas) — Upgrade to $ave
**Citation:** Ouachita Electric Cooperative. "Upgrade to $ave Program."
**Access:** https://www.oecc.com/upgrade-to-save (or the Cooperative's current program page)
**Role:** Companion deployment to Roanoke — same PAYS-modeled tariff in a different state. Comparison data point.

### Midwest Energy (Kansas / Nebraska) — How$mart
**Citation:** Midwest Energy, Inc. "How$mart Program."
**Access:** https://www.mwenergy.com/howsmart
**Role:** Investor-owned utility implementation of an on-bill tariff (vs. cooperative model). Useful for understanding rate-base treatment.

### Center for EcoTechnology (CET) & Ipswich Utilities (MA) — Resource Ipswich
**Citation:** Hoch, L. & Muspratt, A. (March 2024). "Unlocking Residential Electrification with Inclusive Utility Investments: Resource Ipswich Final Report." Center for EcoTechnology.
**Access:** https://www.cetonline.org/ (search "Resource Ipswich")
**Role:** Most recent (2024) TOB pilot focused on residential **electrification** rather than just EE — directly relevant to PV+storage extension.

### Inclusive Utility Investments — White paper
**Citation:** "Inclusive Utility Investments: Tariffed On-Bill Programs." Clean Energy Works (or similar organizing publisher).
**Access:** https://www.cleanenergyworks.org/ (or https://eepartnership.org/)
**Role:** Framing of TOB as an "inclusive" investment vehicle that bypasses traditional credit screening.

### Flexible Financial Credit Agreements — Issue brief
**Citation:** "Flexible Financial Credit Agreements: Tariff On-Bill Financing (TOBF)."
**Access:** [verify — possibly EESI, CESA, or a state energy office issue brief]
**Role:** Brief overview used as background reading.

## 4. NC-specific regulatory documents

### Duke Energy Carolinas — Schedule PP (Purchased Power), NC
**Citation:** Duke Energy Carolinas, LLC. "Schedule PP (NC) — Purchased Power." Electricity No. 4, North Carolina Fourteenth Revised Leaf No. 90 (superseding Thirteenth Revised Leaf No. 90). Effective November 1, 2023. NCUC Docket No. E-100, Sub 194 (order dated September 30, 2024).
**Access:** Search NCUC docket browser at https://starw1.ncuc.gov/ for Docket E-100, Sub 194; or Duke Energy tariff library at https://www.duke-energy.com/our-company/regulators/electricity-tariffs.
**Role:** Avoided cost / purchased-power schedule used as the floor for export compensation in our NC TOBF cash flows.

### North Carolina Utilities Commission — Approved Rate of Return
**Citation:** North Carolina Utilities Commission (NCUC). "Approved Rate of Return — North Carolina."
**Access:** Search the NCUC docket browser at https://starw1.ncuc.gov/ for the most recent rate-case order applicable to Duke Energy Carolinas.
**Role:** Authorized utility rate of return used as a sanity check on TOBF tariff capital recovery assumptions.

## 5. Academic literature (citation-only — paywalled)

### Wang, Negash & Kirschen (2015) — Energy Policy
**Citation:** Wang, Z., Negash, A., & Kirschen, D. (2015). "Assessing the financial impacts of distributed energy on load serving entities." *Energy Policy*, 86, 380–392.
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enpol.2015.06.036
**Publisher:** Elsevier — © 2015. Paywalled; accessible via institutional subscription, ResearchGate preprint, or interlibrary loan.
